# Hello, World! 🌍 👋

I'm Jen Kauppila (pronouced like "a cappella" 🎶), a full stack software engineer, specializing in backend development. This makes sense once you know my brain naturally thinks in systems, which is both a blessing and a curse...

On the one hand, I see the possibility for a better mousetrap pretty much everywhere I turn. But that also means I can't stop noticing when something isn't working the way it should... especially when I know exactly how to fix it.

I run **[Hello World Studio](https://helloworldstud.io)** out of Portland, where I help small and mid-size businesses put AI to work without it becoming one more thing they have to master. The goal is to automate repetitive tasks with durable systems instead of band-aid fixes, allowing people the freedom to get back to doing the things they love to do the most.

## How I think about AI

I love AI. But I also have a very healthy respect for it... kind of like the water.

Born and raised in the south, I've been around water my whole life and have always been a strong swimmer. But I know better than to turn my back on the ocean here in the PNW.

_Why?_ Because as much as I love the water, I also know how dangerous it can be and that it will absolutely humble you the second you forget that.

Knowing _what_ can go wrong is every bit as important as making sure everything else goes right. That's why I'm a firm believer in having an in-depth understanding of how AI works behind the scenes before building with it.

## Current Projects
